## Compatibility interpretation
The supplied matrix is the existing MODEL eligibility matrix, not a factual map of permitted routes. 1 means allowed in the simulation. 0 means excluded by model assumptions, not impossible or prohibited. The scheduled track examples support plausibility at service-group level; departures do not establish where trains came from, exact train formation/length, signals, approach routes, switch conflicts, or permission to reassign a specific train. A scheduled departure on track 5 in a particular section is not proof that every RE1 southbound can use that track.

Examples for every allowed group/track pair are recorded in timetable_examples.csv across two timetable revisions. Do not claim “12/12 confirmed operationally.” Other real trains may occupy tracks 2–5, and real services can use other tracks. Baseline representative tracks are synthetic. If timetable integration is planned, include all traffic sharing modeled tracks or clearly state excluded traffic and test sensitivity to restrictions.
